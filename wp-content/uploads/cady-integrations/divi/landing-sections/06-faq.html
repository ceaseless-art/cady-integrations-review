Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is there an extra cost for integrations?

It depends on the app. Many apps that work directly come with the right phone system plan. Some, like Salesforce, carry a per-user fee, and apps that need an add-on can have their own cost. We'll tell you exactly before anything is set up.

Who sets the integration up?

Cady's team does. We configure and test it with you, then support it after it goes live.

What if my software isn't listed?

Book a call anyway. Many apps can still connect through middleware or a custom API, and we'll find out for you, free of charge.

What's the difference between "works directly" and "works with an add-on"?

"Works directly" means your phone system connects to the app with nothing in between. "Works with an add-on" means a separate connector app sits between the two. Cady sets up both; the add-on can have its own cost.

Do you support Mitel?

Yes. Cady is a Mitel Gold Partner, and the explorer lists the integrations in Mitel's own apps and integrations gallery. If your software isn't listed, Mitel's open APIs often make a custom connection possible. Book a call and we'll check.

Can I share what I find with my team?

Yes. Every app and every pairing in the explorer has its own link. Use "Copy link to this page" and send it to whoever needs to see it.
